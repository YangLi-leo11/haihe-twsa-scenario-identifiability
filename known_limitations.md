# Known limitations

1. Figure 4 map rendering requires a separately obtained HydroBASINS v1c Asia
   level-9 boundary. Numerical source tables are included.
2. Raw groundwater observations and well coordinates are excluded because their
   use and redistribution are subject to the originating monitoring authority's
   conditions. Summary statistics and the bootstrap specification needed to verify
   reported values are included.
3. Full GRACE/GRACE-FO and CMIP6 archives remain with their original providers and
   are not included in this compact release.
